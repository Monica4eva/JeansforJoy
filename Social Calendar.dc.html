Jeans for Joy
60-post social calendar

Every post written and ready to schedule, 1 September – 5 December 2026. Four events, four audiences, one escalating cadence.

How this calendar is built

Event promotion works on an escalating cadence — weekly in the early weeks, near-daily in the final week before each event. This calendar runs roughly 4 posts a week at the start and rises to daily in the seven days before the Parade. Each post is tagged with the persuasion framework driving it, so you can see the arc rather than just the dates.

PASProblem → Agitate → SolveCause posts, donation asks, the "why this exists" content

BABBefore → After → BridgeTransformation stories, upcycling, beneficiary outcomes
Social proofShow others acting firstPhotos of real turnout, teams signing up, sponsor announcements

ReciprocityGive value before askingStyling tips, business advice, behind-the-scenes access

The content mix

Four pillars, balanced across the 60 posts so the feed never reads as one long ask:

40%
Sell — tickets, stalls, teams, seats
25%
Inspire — mothers, stories, the cause
20%
Educate — where money goes, how it works
15%
Engage — polls, questions, UGC prompts

Standing rules for every post

  • One call to action per post. Never two.
  • Link in bio on Instagram; direct link on Facebook. WhatsApp +267 74 310 425 is always the fallback.
  • Post Tue/Thu/Sat 12:00–13:00 or 18:00–20:00 — lunch and after-work are when Gaborone scrolls.
  • Reply to every comment within two hours. The algorithm rewards it and buyers ask questions in comments.
  • Never publish a mother's face or story without written consent. Pride, never pity.
  • Core tags on all: #JeansForJoy #SingleMothersSafeHaven #Gaborone #Botswana

10 · Thu 17 Sep Facebook + Instagram Reciprocity

Three things to check before you donate a pair of jeans to anything — ours included. 🧵

1. Check the seams, not the fade. Faded denim is fashion. Blown seams are landfill.
2. Heavier is better. Thick, rigid denim upcycles into bags and jackets. Stretchy jeggings mostly don't.
3. Wash it first. Obvious. Frequently ignored.

Botswana throws away tonnes of wearable clothing a year while mothers three suburbs over are buying fabric they can't afford.

Bring your best pair to the Workshop on 26 September. We'll teach you what to do with it.

36 · Sat 24 Oct Instagram Carousel Reciprocity

How to pitch your business in 60 seconds. Save this — you'll need it on 14 November. 🎤

0–10s: What you sell, to whom. "I make school uniforms for private primaries in Gaborone." Not your life story. Not yet.

10–25s: The proof. One number. Orders per month, repeat customers, biggest client.

25–45s: The bottleneck. What's actually stopping you growing — machine, capital, shelf space, a buyer.

45–60s: The ask. Be specific. "I need a retailer willing to stock 50 units." People can't help with vague.

Practise it on someone who doesn't care about you. If they get it, it works.
