This isn't a logo placement. It's a movement you help build.

Jeans for Joy is a six-month campaign for Single Mothers Safe Haven Trust, built on fashion, creativity, community, and advocacy. Every partner who joins us is funding something specific: the sewing machines, the training materials, the stage for a first design, and the Fridays that turn spare change into a mother's fresh start.

Why We Do This

Four Pillars. One Movement.

Everything in Jeans for Joy — every event, every stitch, every partnership conversation — sits under one of these four ideas. This is the lens we ask every partner to see the campaign through.

Fashion

Denim as the fabric that crosses every class, age, and style line — the universal language that pulls a community in.

Creativity

A worn pair of jeans becomes a bag, a jacket, a first collection. What people make here, they carry forward.

Community

Schools, companies, designers, and neighbours, all showing up for the same Friday, the same mother, the same cause.

Advocacy

The mission spoken plainly and with pride — never pity — so the people behind it are seen as capable, not needy.

Where It Goes

Proceeds fund people, not overhead.

Single Mothers Safe Haven Trust already has qualified trainers and a vetted group of mothers ready to learn. What's missing is the equipment. That's the gap Jeans for Joy exists to close.

01

Mothers mastering a tradeVetted single mothers building real, sellable skills in tailoring and sewing — the tools to turn fashion into a livelihood, not just a hobby.

02

Young designers finding a stageStudents and emerging designers get their first paying commission or their first public showcase, at the Market and on the Parade route.

03

Children watching it happenBehind every mother in a training programme is a household — kids who see, up close, what it looks like when their mother builds something of her own.

04

A community that shows upEvery Friday, every workshop seat, every ticket is someone choosing to be part of it — joy, given back, on purpose.

How We Partner

We start with a conversation, not a menu.

There's no fixed list of open sponsor slots here — packages are flexible, and we'd rather understand what your organisation can genuinely offer and build something around that, together. If you're interested, get in touch and that's exactly where we'll start.

First

We talk about your brand

A short conversation about what matters to you, what you're able to contribute, and where it might fit naturally into the campaign.

Then

We agree it in writing

What you'll give, what you'll receive, and exactly where your name will appear — confirmed before anything is promised publicly.

Throughout

We report back honestly

You'll hear what your partnership made possible in plain terms, including a short impact note after the events you're part of.

Ready When You Are
