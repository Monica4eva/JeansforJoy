On the day

More than a ticket.

You're buying a day out, not a donation receipt. Here's what's waiting at the Market and the Parade — and what the winners walk away with.

Prizes to be won

Best Team Look, Most Creative Individual, Most Cultural Expression and best upcycled design — each with a sponsor-backed prize and a trophy moment on stage.

Hampers, retail vouchers and sponsor goodie bags handed out through the day, plus spot prizes for the boldest denim in the crowd.

A supervised kids' corner, family-priced food stalls run by SMSH's own catering trainees, and seating you can actually sit down at.

Live music, a DJ, dance and cultural performances between the judging rounds, running into the Parade after-party.

Winners are announced on stage at each event and on @singlemotherssafehaven_. The full prize line-up grows as sponsors come on board.

What do I wear?

Denim, however you own it. The Parade rewards the boldest looks, but jeans and a good attitude get you in anywhere. Mama's Table is jeans too — that's the whole point of it.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes to the Market and the Parade — there's a kids' corner, family catering and a P25 kids' rate, with under-5s free. The Workshop takes under-16s at P200 with an adult. Mama's Table is adults only.

Do I need to know how to sew?

No. The Workshop is led by trainers and built for complete beginners — bring one old pair of jeans and they'll walk you through the rest. Tools and materials are in the ticket.

What if I can't get ten people for a team?

A team is up to ten, not exactly ten — four friends can enter as a team. Or come as an individual at P100 and still compete for Most Creative Individual.

Who can apply for Mama's Table?

Single mothers running a business, from side-hustle to growing. Apply free through the form; seats are limited and confirmed by the campaign team before payment.

How do I know the money reaches mothers?

Funds go to Single Mothers Safe Haven Trust (TUHGB-000021-24) and buy equipment and start-up packs, recorded against named beneficiaries. Each event gets a short impact note, and the campaign a close-out report.

Will I be photographed?

These are public events with photo and video crews, and images may be used in campaign and sponsor content. Tell the registration desk if you'd rather not appear, and we'll respect it — beneficiary stories are only ever published with written consent.

Can I donate without attending?

Always. Bank transfer, Orange Money, Remitly from abroad, or a donation box at any event — all listed below. Sponsor a mother's seat at Mama's Table for P350 if you'd like your gift tied to something specific.
